HOW TO PLAY

Get three marks in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — to win.

Tap any empty square to place your mark. X always moves first.

VS COMPUTER: You play X, the computer plays O. Select EASY, NORMAL or HARD. HARD uses an unbeatable strategy.

TWO PLAYERS: Hand the same phone or computer to your friend and take turns as X and O. No account or internet connection is required.

Classic BASIC editions also used numbered squares (1–9); on a keyboard, you may press 1–9 to move.
